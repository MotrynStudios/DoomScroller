# Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in DoomScroller600 to be respectful and useful to people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and accessibility needs.

Examples of welcome behavior include clear, constructive feedback, patience with questions, and discussing ideas rather than attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when needed. To report a concern, contact the maintainer through <https://github.com/theinevitableinvincible/DoomScroller600> or [DM on Discord](https://discord.com/users/543177724450308136). Do not post private information publicly.
